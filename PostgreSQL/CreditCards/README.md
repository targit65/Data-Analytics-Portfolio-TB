Credit Card Analytics Dashboards

A collection of interactive Power BI dashboards designed to visualize key insights from credit card transaction and customer data.
These dashboards provide a 360° view of business performance, customer demographics, spending behavior, and revenue trends.
Dashboard 1: Card Transaction Dashboard

This dashboard highlights overall business performance and card usage insights.

Key Metrics:

Total Revenue: 55M

Total Clients: 10K

Total Transactions: 45M

Total Interest: 7.8M

Insights Covered:

Category Overview: Transaction, Revenue & Interest across Blue, Silver, Gold, and Platinum cards.

Revenue Analysis: By Quarter, Age Group, Profession, Education Level, and Expense Type.

Usage Behavior: Revenue by Chip Type (Swipe, Chip, Online).

Demographic Filters: Gender, Marital Status, and Quarter-based segmentation.

Tools & Techniques:

Power BI for interactive visualization

Data Modeling and Relationship building

DAX for KPI creation (Revenue, Interest, Transaction Volume)

Custom Filters and Slicers for data exploration

Dashboard 2: Credit Card Customer Dashboard

This dashboard focuses on customer behavior, income distribution, satisfaction rating, and ownership patterns.

Key Metrics

Total Revenue: 55M

Total Clients: 10K

Total Transactions: 45M

Total Interest: 7.8M

Insights Covered:

Customer Profile: House Owner Ratio, Car Owner Ratio, and Marital Status.

Demographics: Income levels, Dependents, and Qualification-based revenue.

Satisfaction Analysis: Customer satisfaction segmented by gender and income.

Revenue Breakdown: By Occupation, State, and Gender.

Comparative View: Gender-wise income and revenue trends over time.

Tools & Techniques

Power BI Desktop for design and data transformation

DAX formulas for performance measures

Data visualization with interactive charts and cards

Segmentation analysis for better decision support
